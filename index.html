Smart lock door measurement checklist

What should I measure before buying a smart door lock?

Record door thickness, lock faceplate size, handle backset, lock and handle spacing, frame clearance and opening direction.

Worked example A sample note could read “45 mm door, 60 mm backset, opens inwards with hinges on the left viewed from outside”. Mark anything you could not measure as unknown.

Limits Send photos of both door faces, the door edge and frame to the installer. Measurements alone cannot confirm model or installation compatibility.

買智能門鎖之前，門身要量邊幾樣？

先記錄門厚、鎖體面板尺寸、門邊到手柄軸中心距離、手柄軸至鎖膽中心距離、門框空間及開門方向。

例子 例如可寫「門厚 45 毫米、門邊到手柄軸中心 60 毫米、從門外看向內開、左鉸」。量不到的項目可標為未量。

適用限制 連同門內、門外、門邊及門框相片交給師傅。實際型號和安裝兼容性需由師傅確認。
